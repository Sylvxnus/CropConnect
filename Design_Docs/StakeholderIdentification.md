## Stakeholder Identification (BD)

For **CropConnect**, the stakeholders were identified by looking at the full process of how surplus allotment produce would move through the system: from growers donating food, to food banks managing it, to residents being able to access it.

This matters because the project is not only about building an app, but about designing something that fits the local issues in **Ladywood**, including:

* Food insecurity
* Transport barriers
* Digital exclusion
* Language barriers

## Justification

These stakeholders were chosen because CropConnect only works if each part of the food redistribution chain is considered.

Growers need to donate surplus produce easily, food banks need to manage it safely, volunteers help reduce transport barriers, and residents need to access support without unnecessary difficulty.

The main design issue is that the people who benefit most from the system may also face the biggest barriers to using it, such as:

* Low digital confidence
* Limited English
* Limited mobile data
* Transport problems

Because of this, the app needs to be simple and low-barrier rather than feature-heavy.

This is why features like **Assisted Access Mode**, **bilingual support**, **map access**, **simple donation forms**, **validation**, and **secure QR credit redemption** are directly linked to stakeholder needs, rather than just being extra features.

## Stakeholders

* Residents
