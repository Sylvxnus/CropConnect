# Agile Methodology Justification

Agile was selected for this project due to its flexibility, collaborative nature, and suitability for iterative software development within a group environment.

The proposed system contains several interconnected features, including food bank stock management, multilingual support, and many more. Due to the complexity of these features and the possibility of requirements evolving throughout development, Agile provides the most suitable approach for this project.

By using Agile, the team was able to divide the project into smaller development stages, allowing features to be designed, implemented, and refined incrementally throughout the project lifecycle. This also supports regular testing, feedback, and communication between team members during all stages of development.

## Agile Project Management

Task allocation and progress tracking were managed using Trello. The board was organised into workflow stages to clearly display planned tasks, tasks currently in progress, and completed work. Responsibilities were distributed evenly amongst team members across documentation, design, and implementation tasks to ensure a balanced workload throughout development. The use of colour coding within the Trello board also improved visibility of ownership and progression throughout the project.

GitHub was used for version control and collaborative development. This allowed team members to work on separate components of the application simultaneously while maintaining structured and recoverable code. Regular communication was maintained through group discussions in the labs and messaging platforms, allowing us to share updates, resolve development issues, and adjust priorities where necessary.

## Benefits of Agile

Agile methodology provided several advantages throughout the project. One of the main benefits was flexibility, as many of the system's user-centred features required ongoing refinement and testing. These features benefitted from testing and continuous improvement rather than being implemented in a fixed sequence.

Agile also improved collaboration between team members by allowing tasks to be divided into smaller manageable components. This made it easier for multiple areas of the project to progress simultaneously without heavily depending on the completion of a single task beforehand.

It also helped to reduce development risk by prioritising core functionality before more advanced features. This ensured that essential components of the application could be developed and tested early before expanding into additional functionality.

## Challenges and Limitations

Despite its advantages, the Agile approach also presented several challenges throughout the project. Changes made to one area of the system could occasionally affect the requirements of another. This meant that there had to be consistent communication between team members to ensure that development remained aligned with the overall project objectives.

Delays in documentation, design, or implementation tasks could impact the progress of other team members whose work relied on that information being available, as multiple sections were worked on simultaneously.

To minimise these challenges, the team relied on regular communication, task tracking through Trello, and version control through GitHub to ensure development remained organised and aligned with project development.
